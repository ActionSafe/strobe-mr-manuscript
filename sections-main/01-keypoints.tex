\textbf{Question:}
Can large language models (LLMs) accurately assess adherence to the STROBE-MR reporting guideline in Mendelian randomization (MR) studies, and what patterns can we observe in MR reporting?

\textbf{Findings:}
The LLM-based approach achieved good agreement with human reviewers and identified substantial variation in reporting quality across STROBE-MR items and sections. No improvement of reporting observed since the introduction of reporting guideline.

\textbf{Meaning:}
LLMs can enable scalable assessment of reporting guideline compliance, providing a practical approach for monitoring reporting quality. Furthur efforts are needed to promote the use of reporting guideline and improve MR reporting.
