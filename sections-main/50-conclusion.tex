In summary, LLMs are a promising tool for large-scale assessing and monitoring of reporting quality. MR studies generally report their background, aims, key results and findings clearly. Significant gaps remain in methodological transparency and reproducibility. Reporting quality has not improved over time and may even be declining in recent years, despite the availability of reporting guidelines. LLM-based approaches offer a promising tool for assessing and monitoring transparent reporting of medical literature at scale. Future efforts can focus on integrating LLM into literature publication workflows and educational intervention to increase engagement of MR researchers with STROBE-MR.
